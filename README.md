# Mostar Građevine
### Adventuring Guild
- Starts at level 1
- 7 levels
	- (---, 300, 500, 900, 1300, 1700, 2500): 8100 Total
- Allows creation of new characters of the buildings level
- Must be upgraded by a character at least two levels higher than the current level
### Alchemist
- Does not exist in the village at the start
- Potions are potent only during the adventure for which they were created
- 3 levels, 5300 gp total
	- 700 gp: lvl 1, start every adventure with one healing potion
	- 1500 gp: lvl 2, start every adventure with two healing potions
	- 3100 gp: lvl 3, start every adventure with two healing potions and a random beneficial potion
### Auction House
- Does not exist in the village at the start
- Enables sale of magic items
- 1 level
	- 500gp: lvl 1
### Blacksmith
- Starts at level 1
- sells weapon, armour and mount gear
- 4 levels, 700 gp total
	- ---: lvl 1, sells items which does not require metal 
	- 200 gp: lvl 2, acquires forge, now sells metal items
	- 500 gp: lvl 3, improves forge, now sells mithral armour and obsidian weapons
### Crypt
- Starts at level 1
- 5 or 6 max levels, depending on what max party size we settle
	- (---, 200, 400, 600, 900, 1200, 1500): 4800 Total
- Max number of active boons equal to building level
- When an adventurer dies during an adventure, their remains (whatever that may be), can be brought to the crypt and interred there. When the adventurer is interred, their player picks a boon from the list below. Before an expedition, the dead adventurer can instil upon another adventurer who prays at their grave gains the boon. An adventurer can only benefit from one boon at a time, and only one adventurer can have that dead adventurer’s boon at a time. If you already have a boon and pray for another, the new one replaces the old one. Expending a boon requires no action. After you expend the boon, you no longer have it.
	- Boon of Disappearance: You can use this boon to become invisible for 1d4 rounds. 
	- Boon of Escape: You can expend this boon to teleport a up to near. 
	- Boon of Haste: You can use this boon to move far on your turn and still take an action for 1d4 rounds.
	- Boon of Flight: You can use this boon to gain a flying speed equal to your speed for 1d4 rounds.
	- Boon of Fury: When you deal damage with an attack, you can expend this boon to deal an additional 1d4 damage to the target.
	- Boon of Greed: You can expend this boon when you are in a dungeon to learn the direction of the most valuable treasure on the same level as the dungeon as you and how many chambers, including hallways, you must pass through to reach them.
	- Boon of the Hunter: You can expend this boon as you kill a significant monster to get xp equal to the monsters level divided by 2.
	- Boon of Luck: You can expend this boon to get a luck token
### General Store
- Starts at level 1
- 1 level
- Allows sale and purchase of basic gear and non magic item treasure
### Stable
- Does not exist in the village at the start
- 3 levels, 1500 gp total
	- 300 gp: lvl 1, allows purchase and storage of common mounts
	- 500 gp: lvl 2, allows purchase and storage of uncommon mounts
	- 700 gp: lvl 3, allows purchase and storage of rare mounts
